# Sustainable Development Goal alignment

## Assessment

This is a limited, indirect alignment with **SDG 9: Industry, Innovation and Infrastructure**, specifically **Target 9.5**, which concerns strengthening scientific research and technological capabilities. The target and its official indicators are described by the [United Nations SDG 9 page](https://sdgs.un.org/goals/goal9).

| Dimension | Assessment |
|---|---|
| Technical contribution | An open, reproducible educational/research benchmark for comparing RUL methods on simulated turbofan trajectories, with evaluation protocols and artifacts. |
| Demonstrated impact | None measured. The repository reports model and software experiments; it does not measure industry adoption, research capacity, or societal outcomes. |
| Potential impact | It may help learners and researchers inspect prognostics workflows and adapt methods for further study. Any broader benefit depends on later independent validation and responsible use. |
| Possible project-level indicators | Number of reproducible runs by independent users, external research use, or citations could be tracked if the maintainer chooses to collect them. These are not UN indicators and are not currently measured. |
| Limitations | Simulated C-MAPSS data only; no operational aircraft records, verified maintenance outcomes, deployment, or evidence of contribution to national R&D capacity. |

The project does not claim to advance SDG 9.4's industrial resource efficiency or emissions outcomes, and no other SDG alignment is sufficiently direct to claim. This is a framing note about the project's research subject, not a claim of sustainable-development impact.
