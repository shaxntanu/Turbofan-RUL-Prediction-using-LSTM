From engine cycles to a remaining-life label

A short, data-grounded walkthrough of the input window, RUL target construction, and validation-calibrated uncertainty used in this project.

Watch the 13-second method animation

Measured signal

The curve is raw sensor_4 data from the bundled FD001 training file. Its vertical display position is scaled for clarity.

Actual training label

Engine 1 ends at cycle 192. At cycle 163, its capped remaining-life label is min(125, 192 - 163) = 29 cycles.

Uncertainty method

The 95% nominal split-conformal radius is calibrated from validation residuals; the interval is bounded to 0-125 cycles.

Interpretation: the regressor and interval are schematic in this animation. It does not report a sample-specific prediction. Nominal conformal coverage is not guaranteed under changes in engines or operating conditions.

Read the text transcript

A measured sensor_4 trace for FD001 engine 1 appears across its 192 cycles. An orange 30-cycle window moves along the trace and stops at cycle 163, covering cycles 134 through 163. The model input contains 15 retained sensors and 3 operating settings for each cycle. The training label at cycle 163 is 29 cycles, calculated from the engine's recorded end at cycle 192 and the 125-cycle cap. A schematic regressor produces an RUL estimate. Its split-conformal interval uses a residual quantile calibrated on validation data at 95% nominal coverage. The interval is clipped to 0-125 cycles. This is a methods illustration, not a measured model prediction, and coverage may change under domain shift.
