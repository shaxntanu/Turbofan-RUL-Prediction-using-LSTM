## Reading the evidence

- NASA identifies the source measurements as simulated. The project and dashboard describe them as simulator data, not flight records.
- The downloaded paper copies are inputs supplied with the repository. The repository MIT license does not override their source-specific reuse conditions.
- The 2025 and 2026 papers inform experiment design and interpretation. They do not validate the C-MAPSS model results as operational performance.
- The 2008 paper is relevant to benchmark provenance. No separate C-MAPSS simulator is bundled or run by this project.
